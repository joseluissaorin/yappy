## Primer comentario del maker

> Hi Product Hunt! I'm José Luis, I study Spanish philology and I read far more
> than I have time to sit down for. Yappy is the reader I wanted: I share
> whatever I'm reading, put the phone in my pocket and keep going while I cook,
> walk or ride the bus.
>
> Three things make it different:
>
> **It's all on your device.** The voice model (Supertonic 3, open, 31
> languages) runs locally. Your texts never leave the phone and it works in
> airplane mode.
>
> **It reads like a person, not a spell-checker.** Before speaking, Yappy turns
> the text into a reading script: dates, Roman numerals, currencies, acronyms and
> abbreviations are said the way a native speaker would say them, in each
> language. That's why the karaoke highlight can follow the voice exactly.
>
> **It's a toy on the outside.** The parrot breathes, blinks, preens when you
> ignore it and opens its beak with the real loudness of the voice. Everything
> is a sticker you can drag around. A six-year-old should want to play with it;
> a sixty-year-old professor should be able to use it every day without the toy
> getting in the way.
>
> It's free on Android and desktop (macOS, Windows, Linux), and the whole code is
> MIT on GitHub. On iPhone three documents fit at a time for free; Yappy
> Parlanchín removes the limit and adds audiobooks and the phone↔computer bridge.
>
> I'd love to hear which language you read in and where the voice trips.
