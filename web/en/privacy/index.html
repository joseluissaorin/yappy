yappy · privacy

Privacy: your texts do not travel.

This is the privacy policy of Yappy (the iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and Linux app, with its widgets and extensions). It is short because there is little to tell: Yappy has no accounts, never asks for your name or email, and what you give it to read never leaves your device.

what we do not collect

No account, no name, no texts.

Yappy has no sign-up and no login. It does not collect your name, email, contacts, location or any advertising identifier. The articles, PDFs, EPUBs, documents, voice notes and texts you share with the app are turned into speech inside your device: synthesis, transcription and page clean-up happen locally and are never sent to any server of ours or of third parties.

what does touch the network

Four cases, all from your device.

Downloading the voices

The first time, Yappy downloads the voice model (about 200 MB on the phone, 380 MB on the desktop) from modelos.yappy.joseluissaorin.com, a store on Cloudflare. As with any download, the server sees your IP address during the request. We keep no log that identifies you.

The links you share

If you share a link or a YouTube video, your device visits that address to fetch the text or the transcript, just as your browser would. The visit is made by your device, not by a Yappy server, and the site receives what it would receive from a browser (your IP, the device type). No history is kept outside your device.

Anonymous statistics (optional)

The iPhone app may send minimal, anonymous usage statistics: which step of the initial walk is seen, which plan is tapped, whether a reading finishes. They never include names, texts, titles, links or advertising identifiers; only a random installation identifier, the language and the app version. They are stored on the author's own server (a Cloudflare Worker and a D1 database), not on third-party services, and they exist to learn which parts of the app get used. Switch them off in the back room ("Anonymous statistics").

Purchases (Yappy Parlanchín)

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. To know whether your parrot is wound up, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription manager, which receives an anonymous installation identifier and the Apple receipt; it does not receive your name or email. Their policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy. Apple describes its own data at apple.com/legal/privacy.

the bridge

From your iPhone to your computer, and back.

If you pair Yappy with your Mac or PC, the documents you send for conversion travel over a direct, end-to-end encrypted connection between your two devices (using the iroh library). To find each other they may rely on relay servers, which only see encrypted traffic they cannot read. Yappy has no intermediate servers that store anything.

the rest

Children, permissions and your rights.

Yappy is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, whatever their age. The permissions the system asks for (clipboard when pasting, camera when scanning the bridge QR code, microphone on the desktop for dictation) are used only for that action, at that moment.

Since we store no personal data, there is nothing to export or delete on our servers: everything Yappy knows about you lives on your device and disappears when you uninstall it. If you have any question, write to jl@joseluissaorin.com or open an issue on GitHub. The code is open source (MIT licence): you can check everything above.
