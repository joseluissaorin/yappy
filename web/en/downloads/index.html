yappy · downloads

Free, open and yours.

Yappy is open source under the MIT license. No accounts, no sign-up, no per-character fee: being able to hear what you read is an accessibility matter, and accessibility should not ask for a credit card.

Android beta on its way

First launch downloads the voice model (~380 MB), once. After that, Yappy works offline forever: synthesis, transcription and article extraction all happen on your device.

requirements

What it takes.

Computer

Any laptop from the last few years synthesizes faster than real time. On Apple Silicon Macs, several times faster.

iPhone and iPad

iOS or iPadOS 17 onwards. It is on the App Store: one tap to install, and the voices come down with the install itself.

Disk

The app itself is small; the voice model takes about 380 MB, and the ear (transcription) downloads separately only if you use it.

Network

Only for the initial model download and for visiting the URLs you share. Everything else works in airplane mode.

for builders

From source.

The whole project lives on GitHub: the desktop and mobile app (Tauri + Rust + Svelte), the synthesis engine, the guionizador with its vendored CLDR grammars, and this very website. With Rust and Node installed, cargo tauri build produces your own copy. Full instructions are in the README.
